# References

Short key, then the source. Add yours in alphabetical order of key; complete entries are in `data/09_papers/_NOTAS.md` and in each data folder's `_NOTAS.md`.

- **[Carr2003]** Carr, C. E., Newman, D. J. and Hodges, K. V. (2003). Apollo 14 EVA energy estimates (NASA 1495/1599 kJ, model 1443/1430 kJ). Full title not checked; cited through [Guide-REV3]. http://web.mit.edu/aeroastro/www/people/dnewman/pdfs/DJN_paper3.43.pdf
- **[Guide-REV3]** Team study guide *Planificador de EVA en Marte: guía de estudio de cero a experto*, technical revision 3, 2026-11-13 (`Guia_zero_a_hero_Planificador_EVA_Marte_2026_REV3.pdf`, shared by the team, not in git). Source of the reference values for E3.
- **[HiRISE-Gale]** MSL Gale HiRISE colour orthophoto mosaic (`MSL_Gale_HiRISE-LRGB_78quads_sharp_cog.tif`), 0.25 m/pixel, and the 25 cm greyscale mosaic (`MSL_Gale_Orthophoto_Mosaic_25cm_v3.tif`).
- **[Johnson2010]** Johnson, A. W., Hoffman, J. A., Newman, D. J., Mazarico, E. M. and Zuber, M. T. (2010). An Integrated Traverse Planner and Analysis Tool for Planetary Exploration. AIAA SPACE 2010, table 2 (Santee et al. 2001 metabolic model for suited astronauts).
- **[Kim2025]** Kim et al. (2025). NASA CSRM Traverse Model, ICES-2025-129, equations 1–8 (Pandolf + Santee 2003 correction, Apollo speed regression).
- **[Parker2016]** Parker, T. J. and Calef, F. J. (2016). MSL Gale Crater DEM mosaic, 1 m/pixel, v3 (HRSC, CTX and HiRISE tied to MOLA), USGS Astrogeology. File used: `MSL_Gale_DEM_Mosaic_1m_v3.tif`.
- **[PLACES]** MSL PLACES localization database, PDS4 bundle `msl_places`, PDS Imaging Node, NASA/JPL (server files dated 2026-08-01). Table used: `data_localizations/localized_interp.csv`; column definitions in `document/PLACES_PDS_SIS.pdf`.
- **[sklearn]** Pedregosa, F. et al. (2011). Scikit-learn: Machine Learning in Python. JMLR 12, 2825–2830.
